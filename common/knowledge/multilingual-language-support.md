Language support differs by product and by mode.

Nova-3 (Speech-to-Text): Supports 51 unique spoken languages with various regional accents through dedicated monolingual models. Also a 10-language multilingual model that can recognize mixed-language conversations and code-switching.

Indic languages(Speech-to-Text): Nova-3 includes dedicated monolingual models for Hindi, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, and Urdu. Of these, only Hindi is included in the 10-language multilingual model.


Flux (Speech-to-Text): Available as English-only or the same 10-language multilingual model as Nova-3, with built-in support for automatic language detection, code-switching, and conversational turn-taking.

Nova-2 (Speech-to-Text): Supports 34 unique spoken languages, plus a 2-language multilingual mode (English and Spanish). 

Aura-2 (Text-to-Speech): Supports 7 languages across its voice catalog.

List of Languages:

- Nova-3 Monolingual: Arabic, Belarusian, Bengali, Bosnian, Bulgarian, Catalan, Cantonese, Mandarin Chinese, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Gujarati, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Kannada, Korean, Latvian, Lithuanian, Macedonian, Malay, Marathi, Norwegian, Persian, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Serbian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swedish, Tagalog, Tamil, Telugu, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, Urdu, Vietnamese

- Nova-3 Multilingual: English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Italian, Dutch

- Flux: English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Italian, Dutch


- Aura-2: English, Spanish, German, French, Dutch, Italian, and Japanese.
